A ritual in three breaths

Every full moon in Hội An, the electric lights go dark and the river fills with paper lanterns carrying people's wishes. Lanterna brings that quiet moment to your screen.

    About Lanterna

    A small, calm place to put your thoughts into light. Made for the Build Your Own Website Hackathon 2026 by Roxten Studios.

    How it's built

    Hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. The sky, stars, mountains, river and lanterns are drawn live on Canvas 2D. The ambient music and chimes are generated with the Web Audio API. There are no images, audio files or frameworks.

    Disclosure

    Built with help from Claude, an AI coding assistant by Anthropic. Fonts come from Google Fonts (Cormorant Garamond and Inter). No templates or third-party JS libraries are used.
